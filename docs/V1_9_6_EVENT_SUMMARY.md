# Engine Arena 1.9.6 — completed-event summaries

Private cumulative test update. Designed by Mark Young. Coded with OpenAI Codex.
The public release remains 1.2.14.

Completed custom competitions now show the correct champion season and clearly
separate the final-match score from cumulative knockout statistics. Championship
tables follow bracket placement, including Champion, Runner-up and tied round
of elimination, rather than implying that total game points decide the winner.

Placement playoffs have group and sequence labels. The overview presents them
in a compact history table instead of a series of indistinguishable panels.
Earlier saved events receive these display labels without rewriting their
results. Original games, attempts, opening pairs and rating contributions remain
intact.

The short season report shows shared time controls and opening-book information
once, uses stage leaders for custom competitions, and omits irrelevant division
movement sections. Complete reports retain every stage table. Career histories
show knockout finishes. Completed placements are no longer described as live
provisional results. Switching data tabs retains useful panel height.

Also includes the Quick match PGN saving correction found during workflow
filming. Quick match no longer tries to read export controls belonging to the
full tournament editor. Existing export destination preferences are respected.

This update includes all 1.9.5 focused-board sound and resizing fixes. No replay
or tournament reset is required.

Pause and drain any active games, close Engine Arena, and run the installer over
the existing installation. Your saved engines, events, games and ratings remain.
