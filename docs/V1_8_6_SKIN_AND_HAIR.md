# Engine Arena 1.8.6 — skin, hair and hallway lighting

Designed by Mark Young. Coded with OpenAI Codex.

Private update for owner review. Includes all previous 1.8 updates.

- The younger seated player's cheeks, forehead and jaw now use her body skin
  texture, retaining the supplied reference's brows and lip details locally.
  This removes the dark photographic shading previously baked into her face.
  Her face, neck and arms continue to respond normally to room lighting.
- Rebuilt the short hairstyles around each model's actual ear positions.
  The temples, sideburns, ear arches and nape form a continuous fitted surface.
  Removed the separate pointed temple strips and oversized ear cutouts.
  Bearded sideburns connect to the existing cheek growth.
- Softened the hair material's highlights. Replaced the hallway point light
  with a diffused ceiling fixture: the old invisible light sat at head height,
  causing an intense highlight when a visitor walked through it.

Visual review covers front, side and rear hair coverage; daylight and evening
skin; and arriving/leaving visitors under the hallway light. The packaged
application checks use disposable data and software WebGL in both workspaces.
