# Engine Arena 1.6.3 — polished boxwood

Private development update. Designed by Mark Young; coded with OpenAI Codex.

White 3D Staunton pieces now have a polished boxwood finish. Lower material
roughness and a stronger, smoother clear finish let the room lighting describe
the carved knight, turned stems, collars and crowns more clearly. The warm wood
color and existing grain remain. The ebony pieces, boards, lighting presets,
sculpture geometry, crown stamps and camera controls retain their prior settings.
The change is automatic in 3D mode; no new preference needs to be enabled.

Four finish variants were compared using the actual renderer. Acceptance checks
cover all six pieces in the four rooms under daylight, tournament and evening
lighting; full positions on all three boards; crown marks; idle rendering; and
3840 × 2160 export. The same views are checked in the packaged application with
isolated test data. This visual update adds no new textures, geometry, render
passes or recurring work. It does not change engine testing or ratings.

Pause and drain, close normally, install 1.6.3, reopen and resume your event.
The update is private and has not been installed or published automatically.
