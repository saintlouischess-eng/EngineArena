# Engine Arena 1.8.5 — character corrections

Designed by Mark Young. Coded with OpenAI Codex.

Private update for owner review. Includes the previous live-club, Tal 1962,
season admission and graphics-recovery changes.

- Corrected the younger seated player's face albedo. Photographic shading was
  being lit again by the room, making her face much darker than her neck and
  arms. The corrected texture retains facial details and uses ordinary room
  lighting; the eyes and other characters' complexions are unchanged.
- Revised all four seated players' resting hand positions and elbow guidance.
  The free hand follows shoulder travel during a lean. Reaching blends from
  the same rest position on either turn, and elbow placement is constrained
  while preserving limb lengths and contact with pieces and clocks.
- Added fitted, tapered temple/sideburn coverage beside the ears. The bearded
  cameos' sideburns connect the hair to the cheek growth, with opaque geometry
  and fine surface strands.

Visual review covers daylight and evening face lighting, front and side seated
poses, and front/side/rear grooming. Motion validation samples the actual twelve
character models through playing, clock presses, walking and bookshelf reading.
It also checks resting elbow placement, bone lengths, hand contact, planted
feet and hip/knee alignment. Final packaged results and checksum are recorded
in the installer's validation sidecar.

Validation uses disposable test data and software WebGL. It does not reproduce
a physical GPU-driver crash or constitute another tournament endurance run.

Pause and drain any testing, then close Engine Arena before installing.
