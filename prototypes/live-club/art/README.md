# Original cameo texture artwork

Created with the built-in image generation tool on 2026-10-11, then projected
and baked onto individually fitted meshes in Blender. Included with Engine
Arena under GPL-3.0-or-later. Source photographs were visual references only,
not copied into the application. These unofficial cameos imply no endorsement.

## Photo references inspected

- [Magnus Carlsen](https://www.chess.com/players/magnus-carlsen): broad cheeks,
  rounded strong jaw, brown swept hair and light stubble.
- [Fabiano Caruana](https://www.chess.com/players/fabiano-caruana): long narrow
  face, prominent nose, dark curls and rectangular black frames.
- [Hans Niemann](https://www.chess.com/players/hans-niemann): tall curly crown,
  youthful longer face and light facial hair.
- [Bobby Fischer, 1972](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bobby_Fischer_1972.jpg):
  long nose/chin, side-parted hair, clean-shaven face and period suit.
- [Hikaru Nakamura](https://www.chess.com/players/hikaru-nakamura): dark parted
  hair, strong eyebrows, wider face and beard.
- [Levy Rozman](https://www.chess.com/players/levy-rozman): tall curly hair,
  narrow face, round frames and short beard/moustache.

The models are artistic interpretations, not exact photographic scans.

## 1.8.8 owner color correction

The owner requested a lighter blond appearance for Magnus. His material now
uses blond locks, a shaded root layer and subtle lighter strands, reviewed in
daylight and evening room lighting. The historical generation prompt below is
retained as provenance; it no longer defines his current hair material.

The original `levy-rook-shirt.svg` and `hikaru-pineapple-shirt.svg` artwork was
drawn for the owner's requested cameo shirts. The caption is "The ROOK!";
the pineapple sits on the wearer's left chest. These are original illustrations,
not copies of merchandise artwork. `scripts/rasterize_club_shirts.cjs` creates
the transparent PNG inputs and `scripts/club_shirt_prints.py` bakes them into
the existing skinned clothing UVs in Blender. The shipped GLBs embed the final
opaque cloth textures; no extra runtime meshes or rendering passes are needed.

## Generation prompts

### guest-face-atlas.png

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Create one original production face-texture
reference atlas for five 3D chess-club spectator cameos. Landscape atlas,
exactly three equal square cells across by two equal square cells down,
seamless light neutral gray background, no borders, NO text or labels. Top row
left to right: Magnus Carlsen (mid-30s, broad cheeks and strong rounded jaw,
thick brown eyebrows, subtle brown stubble); Fabiano Caruana (early 30s, narrow
oval face, long prominent nose, dense dark short curls, WITHOUT his glasses so
3D spectacles can be placed separately); Hans Niemann (early 20s, distinctive
youthful longer face, hazel eyes, curly brown hair, light patchy facial stubble).
Bottom row left to right: Bobby Fischer as in 1972 (29 years old, clean-shaven,
long angular nose, strong cheekbones, distinctive long chin and wider mouth,
neat brown side part); Hikaru Nakamura (late 30s, short dark parted hair, thick
eyebrows, full short black beard and moustache, characteristic face); last
bottom-right cell empty neutral gray. Every occupied cell is a perfectly
straight-on passport-like head portrait, orthographic 135mm view, level eyes
looking directly forward, neutral relaxed closed-mouth expression, full head
and ears visible, cropped at base of neck, no clothes, absolutely no tilt or
three-quarter pose. All faces aligned: top of hair at 8 percent cell height;
eyes at 43 percent cell height; bottom of chin at 83 percent height. Highly
recognizable faithful facial likenesses and natural skin pores. Even flat
cross-polarized studio illumination for texture projection onto 3D models: no
dramatic light, no highlights, no cast shadows, no background scene. This is
original texture artwork, not photographs or implied endorsement.

### levy-face.png

Use case photorealistic-natural. One original square 1024 by 1024 production
face texture portrait of IM Levy Rozman, GothamChess, age 30, for projection
onto a 3D spectator cameo in a chess game. Faithful recognizable facial
likeness: tall narrow forehead, slim oval face, distinctive expressive thick
dark eyebrows, long straight slightly curved nose, blue-gray eyes, short dark
brown beard and moustache, curly dark brown hair high on top and short at
sides. WITHOUT GLASSES: his round glasses will be modeled separately in 3D.
Perfect straight-on orthographic passport portrait, neutral relaxed closed
mouth, eyes level directly facing camera, full hair and both ears visible.
Head occupies most of image, cropped at neck with no clothes, no hands.
Neutral light gray background. Soft even cross-polarized albedo illumination,
natural skin pores, no shadows or harsh highlights. Eye centers approximately
(408,450) and (616,450), bottom of chin about y=860, top of hair y=70. No text,
logos, watermark, frames, or extra people. Original synthetic texture artwork,
not a photograph.

Output sizes were 1536×1024 and 1254×1254. Projection landmarks in
`scripts/build_club_cast.py` use the actual generated dimensions.
