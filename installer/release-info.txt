Engine Arena 1.12.6

1.12.6 polishes the broadcast Moves & thinking panel for television and phone
viewing. Engine-reported NPS appears beside depth, with compact K/M units and
the exact speed in its tooltip; unavailable speed shows a dash. Long engine
names shorten without pushing the search metrics out of view.

The embedded history graph and opening-book information line have been removed
from this panel, leaving more room for larger move and thinking text. Dedicated
graph panels remain available in Broadcast Designer. Saved layouts, tournament
results, ratings, opening assignments and broadcast settings are preserved.

1.12.5 improves broadcast watchability: the finished board stays visible while
waiting for the next fresh game. The game panel shows move/side context, short
played/PV lines, matchup standings and both engines' evaluation history.
Evaluation numbers are horizontal; shared thread labels and shorter development
hashes improve tables without changing stored identities. The completion estimate
uses a steadier rolling window when available, with a five-minute display hold.

Close-game selection now uses exact-profile Elo gaps when ratings are available,
with standings as a fallback. It still follows each selected game from opening to
result. Known opening metadata and explicit engine-analysis attribution are
preserved in the broadcast feed.

1.12.4 adds both engine ratings to result ticker headlines, including engines
outside the top 20. Rating labels are bold and readable; missing estimates show
Elo pending. Upset text explicitly describes the opponent's higher rating,
not a rating gain. Broadcast standings and ratings are uniformly bold, and the
2D thinking arrow is slimmer while preserving its selected color and delay.

Open OBS from Broadcast Designer > OBS program output & switching to start it
at verified Normal Windows process priority. An already-open OBS is reused.
The launcher preserves profiles/scenes and never starts streaming or recording.
It remains independent of Codex. A custom OBS path can be saved if necessary.

1.12.3 redesigns the broadcast header with a larger adjustable logo, organized
progress statistics, a live progress bar and estimated time remaining. Paused,
draining, disconnected and completed events have explicit status messages.
The final score now appears in a ribbon below the board, preserving the entire
final position and keeping the board size stable during the result hold.

1.12.2 adds separate broadcast column-heading and detail-text sizes, larger
readable defaults, and visible text-fit feedback. Main text auto-fitting can
be disabled; the private designer warns about overflow. Saved layouts preserve
these choices. A 2D board grid fix prevents clipped bottom-rank pieces.
The Live/game-status indicator now shares the last-move footer, freeing more
vertical space for the featured chessboard.
Evaluation-bar width and score text size are adjustable per show or scene;
the board automatically fits the remaining space in 2D and 3D.
Engine Elo badges beside the featured players are larger and bold.
Completion percentage and engine count are larger and higher contrast, with
independent sizing through the header panel's detail-text setting.

1.12.1 fixes ticker speed buttons not refreshing the designer preview, clarifies
scene/default ticker settings, and changes speed without jumping the text.

Workflow polish: dedicated Ticker tab, immediate control previews, automatic
draft saving, expanded preview, section navigation, output/pending status,
working panel layers/colors, duplicate panels, consistent keyboard nudging,
result-list paging, and saved-layout/image round trips.

Broadcast Designer: private preview, saved scenes, movable/resizable panels,
12 scene presets, configurable standings/ratings, event playlists, both-engine
graphs with individual colors/styles, and the complete 2D/3D appearance library.

Ticker presets and speed controls, separate colors for scores and game endings,
custom messages, separators and spacing. Visible text finishes its pass before
updating. Normal scene/event changes preserve complete-game coverage.

Pause and drain running games, then close Engine Arena before installing.
End a live broadcast before updating its helper. The Broadcast workspace offers
a confirmed helper restart if an earlier version is running. OBS keeps its URL.
Remove old OBS Custom CSS after transferring its colors to the designer.

See docs/BROADCAST_DESIGNER.md for workflows and data-source limits.
