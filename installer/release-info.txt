Engine Arena 1.9.7 - release-check candidate

Designed by Mark Young. Coded with OpenAI Codex.

Opening replay labels show the opening move number instead of negative
engine-relative ply counts. Includes the completed-event summary fixes.
Keeps creation order when adding same-timestamp tournaments to the queue.
Updates the bundled multidict library to its patched 6.9.1 release.

Corrects the champion season and distinguishes the final-match score from
cumulative knockout standings. Placement playoffs have distinct group and
sequence labels and a compact history table. Reports show shared conditions
once, retain complete stage tables, and show recorded knockout placements.

Fixes Quick match saving when the full editor's PGN controls are not present.
Includes the 1.9.5 focused-board sound and resizing fixes.

Add new engines to a completed standalone round robin, preserving its earlier
results and scheduling only the missing matchups. Preview the game count first;
the extension saves paused. Event stages, Swiss and knockout cannot be extended.

Focused Game now shows a fifty-move rule counter, or the automatic seventy-five
move limit when claims are disabled. Hide it in Board & clocks if desired.
Includes the custom-season overview refresh fix and the 1.9.3 CPU rating-pair
compatibility correction. No replay is required for the rating correction.

Pause and drain active games, close Engine Arena, then install. Existing data
is retained. This private candidate has not been published to GitHub.
